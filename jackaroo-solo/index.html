      Card Reference

      CardAction
      AceHome → Base, or move a marble 1 or 11 steps forward
      KingMove a marble from Home to Base
      QueenMove a marble 12 steps forward
      JackMove any one marble exactly 1 step forward — once per marble, per game
      10 / 9 / 8 / 6 / 3 / 2Move a marble that many steps forward
      7Split 7 steps across two different marbles on the track
      5Move a marble 5 steps backward (reposition for a better entry)
      4Move a marble 4 steps backward

      A marble on Base is safe from the Jack's reposition. Backward moves stop at Base — they never enter Home. You may not play a forward card that would overshoot the Safe Zone.

      House rule: if a selected card has no legal move for any marble, you may discard it unplayed and draw a replacement — that draw still counts toward your score. If every card in your hand is unplayable at once, the app discards the whole hand and deals a fresh one automatically. This keeps the puzzle from ever getting stuck.

      Scoring

      Every card drawn counts — the initial hand of 4, every refill, and a +1 penalty each time the discard pile is reshuffled into a new draw pile. Finish all four marbles in as few draws as possible.
